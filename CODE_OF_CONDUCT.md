# Code of Conduct

Rabenregatta is about people discovering what they can do. That only works in a friendly, safe place. It applies online, in this repository, and at every event.

## We

- welcome everyone, whatever their age, background, skills or abilities.
- praise effort and ideas, not only results.
- help without taking over. **We never build someone else's boat for them.**
- treat the creek, the plants and the animals with respect.
- give and take feedback kindly.

## We do not

- laugh at a boat that sinks. Sinking is part of learning.
- harass, insult or exclude anyone.
- share photos or personal data of others without consent.

## If something goes wrong

At an event, talk to anyone wearing the organizer vest. Online, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately. We will listen and act.
