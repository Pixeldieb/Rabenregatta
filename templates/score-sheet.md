## Criteria

### 💡 Clever Raven (creativity)

- Original idea, unusual solution
- Clever use of a principle (e.g. a smart keel, an unusual float)
- The builder can explain **why** the boat is built this way
- Visible learning: the builder improved the boat after testing

### ♻️ Treasure Raven (reuse)

- Share of reused and natural materials (green and yellow lists)
- Surprising "second life" for an object
- No new materials bought
- Nothing can come off into the creek

## Tips for the jury

- Judge the **idea and the effort**, not how "professional" it looks. A child's boat is not a carpenter's boat.
- Compare boats **within** their age group.
- Have your scores ready before the finals start. Add up the scores of all jury members.
- In case of a tie, the youngest builder wins.
