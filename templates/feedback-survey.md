> **Organisers:** These questions are simple and **not validated**. They show tendencies. To measure self-efficacy properly, use a validated scale such as the General Self-Efficacy Scale ([Schwarzer & Jerusalem, 1995](../wiki/Sources.md#schwarzer-1995)) before and after.
