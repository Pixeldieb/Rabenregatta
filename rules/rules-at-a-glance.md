# 🐦‍⬛ Rabenregatta: The Rules in 7 Points

*Print this page and hang it up in the workshop.*

---

### 1. 📄 Small boat

Your boat must fit on **one sheet of A4 paper**.
It may be up to **42 cm high**, including the mast.

### 2. ✋ Built by you

**You** have the idea. **You** build it.
Grown-ups may help with tools. But the ideas are yours!

### 3. 🌱 Good for the creek

Use **old things** and **natural things**.
Corks, wood, cartons, bottles, string, cloth.

### 4. 🚫 Not allowed

❌ Styrofoam ❌ Glitter ❌ Balloons ❌ Batteries

### 5. 🌊 Powered by nature

Only the **water** and the **wind** move your boat.
No pushing, no blowing, no motors.

### 6. 👀 Hands off

During the race you **do not touch** your boat.
If it gets stuck, the ravens help.

### 7. 🧹 Leave no trace

Every boat and every piece comes **out of the water** again.

---

**Sinking is okay!** Every boat that sinks teaches us something. 💙

**Prizes for:** 🏁 the fastest · 💡 the cleverest · ♻️ the best reuse · ⚓ the toughest · ❤️ the audience favourite
