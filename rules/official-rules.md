# Rabenregatta – Official Rules

**Version:** 1.2 · **Event:** _[Village name, date]_ · **Contact:** _[Name, phone, e-mail]_

> Organisers: adapt the parts in _[brackets]_ to your event. Keep the spirit: self-built, planet-friendly, leave no trace, free for children.

---

## 1. Spirit of the race

The Rabenregatta is about building something yourself and finding out what you can do. Fairness, creativity, community and respect for nature matter more than winning.

## 2. Who can take part

2.1 Everyone.

2.2 Classes:

| Class | Who | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Kids and teens | 0–18, alone, in teams or with their family | free |
| Grown-up ravens | over 18 | _[€3]_ start fee |

2.3 **Taking part is free for all children and teenagers aged 0–18.** This includes building stations, workshops and materials.

2.4 Every participating child receives **one** ice cream voucher, no matter how many boats it registers. Grown-ups do not receive ice cream vouchers.

2.5 Everyone may register **as many boats as they like**.

## 3. The boat

3.1 **Size.** The whole boat must fit on a **DIN A4 footprint (21.0 × 29.7 cm)**. This is checked with the official A4 gauge. There is **no height limit**. _[Optional: add one if your boards are low.]_

3.2 **Self-built.** The boat must be designed and built by its builder(s).
- Not allowed: kits, bought boats, downloaded or copied plans, boats from previous events.
- Allowed: learning building principles from tutorials, books or others; being inspired and then building your own version.
- Adults may help children with safety and with physically very hard steps (e.g. sawing thick wood). The ideas and the design must come from the child.

3.3 **Built on site or brought along.** Boats can be built at the building stations on race day, or built beforehand and brought along.

3.4 **Materials.** Only materials from the green and yellow lists of the [Materials traffic light](../wiki/05-Materials-Traffic-Light.md) are allowed. Yellow materials must be reused and firmly attached. Materials are donated, collected as litter or found in nature.

3.5 **Forbidden:** Styrofoam/polystyrene, glitter, balloons, batteries, motors, electronics, solvent-based paints or varnish, oil or grease, loose small parts.

3.6 **Propulsion.** The boat may only be moved by the current and the wind.

3.7 **Name.** Each boat has a name. The boat is registered and announced by its name, not by the names of its builders.

3.8 **Number.** Each boat carries its start number, firmly attached and clearly visible.

## 4. Registration

4.1 **Only registered boats race.** Boats are registered at the race office, in **separate lists** for kids and teens (0–18) and for grown-ups (over 18).

4.2 At registration, each boat is checked on the A4 gauge, checked for materials, **weighed, measured and photographed**. These values count for the prizes.

4.3 **After registration, a boat may not be changed any more.**

4.4 Registered boats wait in the **gallery** until they are called. No one touches the boats in the gallery.

4.5 Each builder (or guardian) confirms the **builder's pledge**.

## 5. The race

5.1 Boats race in **heats of three**, in the **order of registration**. The race office calls each heat. Grown-ups race in their own block _[after the kids' races]_.

5.2 **Course.** The course includes chicanes set up by sponsors. Chicanes are part of the course.

5.3 **Start.** The builders hold their boats above the water at the start board. On the signal, they **let them drop**. Only dropping is allowed: no pushing, no throwing, no hands in the water.

5.4 **Finish and time.** A boat has finished when its front passes the finish line. Every boat's time is recorded.

5.5 **During the race, no one touches the boats**, blows at them or influences them in any way, except for the rescue in 5.6.

5.6 **Rescue.** Once per race, a boat may be freed or set upright, **only by its own builders and only from the bank**. Nobody may step into the creek to do so. Builders use the raven stick provided.

5.7 **Disqualification.** A boat that sinks, or that gets stuck or capsizes again after its rescue and does not finish, is disqualified for this race. _[Optional: a boat that has not finished after 10 minutes is disqualified.]_

5.8 Stuck or sunk boats and lost parts are recovered after the heat by the course helpers. Builders stay on the bank.

5.9 _[Optional: The three fastest boats race again in a final.]_

## 6. Prizes

6.1 All prizes and their win conditions are announced **months before the event** on every announcement.

6.2 **Fixed prizes:**
- **Fastest Raven:** fastest race time
- **Audience Raven:** most talers in the audience vote

6.3 **Sponsor prizes:** Sponsors choose the win condition of their prize (e.g. slowest boat to finish, heaviest boat).

6.4 Unless stated otherwise, a boat must **finish its race** (not disqualified) to win a prize.

6.5 **Audience vote:** Every participant receives one gold taler, every spectator one silver taler. Both count as one vote. One taler per person. Talers go into the container with the number of the chosen boat in the gallery. You may not vote for your own boat.

6.6 **Ties** are decided by _[the audience vote / lot]_.

6.7 Every boat receives a certificate.

6.8 The race office and the jury decide on all questions. Their decisions are final.

## 7. Nature and safety

7.1 **Leave no trace.** Nothing stays in the creek. All boats are recovered at the catch net. All chicanes are removed on the same day.

7.2 Only official helpers enter the water.

7.3 Children under 10 are accompanied by an adult near the water.

7.4 Follow the instructions of the organisers. In case of thunderstorm or high water, the race is stopped or postponed.

7.5 Stay on paths and marked banks. Respect plants and animals.

## 8. Fair play

8.1 Be kind. Cheer for all boats, especially those that sink.

8.2 Breaking the rules on purpose can lead to disqualification. The organisers decide.

8.3 Protests go to the race office within 10 minutes after the race.

## 9. Photos and data

9.1 Every boat is photographed at registration. Boat photos, boat names and boat data (weight, height, time, prizes) may be published, for example in the online boat gallery. Names of builders are not published.

9.2 Photos showing people are only published with consent. Details: see the photo consent form.
