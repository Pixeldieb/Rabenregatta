## 1. Spirit of the race

The Rabenregatta is about building something yourself and finding out what you can do. Fairness, creativity and respect for nature matter more than winning.

## 2. Who can take part

2.1 Everyone aged 6 and over. Children under 6 can take part in a family team.

2.2 Age groups:

| Group | Age |
|---|---|
| Raven chicks | 6–9 |
| Young ravens | 10–13 |
| Ravens | 14 and over |
| Raven families | Mixed teams with at least one child under 14 |

2.3 A builder may enter **one boat** per age group. Teams have at most 3 members (families: no limit).

2.4 Registration by _[date]_ using the registration form. Participation is _[free]_.

## 3. The boat

3.1 **Size.** The whole boat, including sails, masts and all parts, must fit into a box of **21.0 × 29.7 × 42.0 cm** (DIN A4 footprint, max. 42 cm high). The boat is checked with the official A4 gauge.

3.2 **Self-built.** The boat must be designed and built by the registered builder(s).
- Not allowed: kits, bought boats, downloaded or copied plans, boats from previous events.
- Allowed: learning building principles from tutorials, books or others; being inspired and then building your own version.
- Adults may help children with safety and with physically very hard steps (e.g. sawing thick wood). The ideas and the design must come from the child.

3.3 **Materials.** Only materials from the green and yellow lists of the [Materials traffic light](../wiki/05-Materials-Traffic-Light.md) are allowed. Yellow materials must be reused and firmly attached.

3.4 **Forbidden:** Styrofoam/polystyrene, glitter, balloons, batteries, motors, electronics, solvent-based paints or varnish, oil or grease, loose small parts.

3.5 **Propulsion.** The boat may only be moved by the current and the wind.

3.6 **Name.** Each boat has a name. Builders present it at registration.

3.7 **Marking.** Each boat carries its start number, visible and firmly attached (provided at registration).

## 4. Check-in

4.1 All boats pass the **gauge check** (size) and the **material check** at registration.

4.2 A boat that does not pass may be changed at the repair station and checked again before its first heat.

4.3 Each builder (or guardian) signs the **builder's pledge**.

## 5. The race

5.1 Boats race in heats of 4–6 boats. The heat plan is posted at the race office.

5.2 **Start.** Boats are placed upstream of the start gate. The starter opens the gate for all boats at once.

5.3 **Finish.** A boat has finished when its front passes the finish line. The finish judges decide the order.

5.4 The first **two** boats of each heat go to the next round. _[Adjust to number of boats.]_ A lucky loser heat may give non-qualified boats a second chance.

5.5 **During the race, builders must not touch their boats**, blow at them or influence them in any way.

5.6 **Stuck boats** may only be freed by official course helpers ("ravens") with a raven stick. A boat that is stuck for more than 2 minutes, or sinks, does not finish this heat.

5.7 **Repairs** between heats are allowed at the repair station. The boat must still meet all rules in section 3.

5.8 If a boat loses parts, the builder helps to recover them after the heat.

## 6. Prizes

6.1 Per age group:
- **Fastest Raven:** winner of the final
- **Clever Raven:** most creative idea (jury)
- **Treasure Raven:** best reuse of materials (jury)
- **Iron Raven:** completes all its heats without repairs (race office)

6.2 **Audience Raven:** favourite boat of the spectators (across all groups).

6.3 Every participant receives a certificate.

6.4 The jury consists of _[3]_ people. Its decisions are final.

## 7. Nature and safety

7.1 **Leave no trace.** Nothing stays in the creek. All boats are recovered at the catch net.

7.2 Only official helpers enter the water.

7.3 Children under 10 are accompanied by an adult near the water.

7.4 Follow the instructions of the organisers. In case of thunderstorm or high water, the race is stopped or postponed.

7.5 Stay on paths and marked banks. Respect plants and animals.

## 8. Fair play

8.1 Be kind. Cheer for all boats, especially those that sink.

8.2 Breaking the rules on purpose can lead to disqualification. The organisers decide.

8.3 Protests go to the race office within 10 minutes after the heat. The jury decides.

## 9. Photos and data

9.1 Photos and videos will be taken at the event. Details and consent: see the registration form.
