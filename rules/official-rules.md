# Rabenregatta – Official Rules

**Version:** 1.1 · **Event:** _[Village name, date]_ · **Contact:** _[Name, phone, e-mail]_

> Organisers: adapt the parts in _[brackets]_ to your event. Keep the spirit: self-built, planet-friendly, leave no trace, free for children.

## 1. Spirit of the race

The Rabenregatta is about building something yourself and finding out what you can do. Fairness, creativity, community and respect for nature matter more than winning.

## 2. Who can take part

2.1 Everyone. Children under 6 take part in a family team.

2.2 Age groups:

| Group | Age | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Raven chicks | 6–9 | free |
| Young ravens | 10–13 | free |
| Teen ravens | 14–17 | free |
| Raven families | Mixed teams with at least one child under 18 | free |
| Grown-up ravens | 18 and over | _[€3]_ start fee |

2.3 **Taking part is free for all children and teenagers aged 0–18.** This includes workshops and materials.

2.4 A builder may enter **one boat** per age group. Teams have at most 3 members (families: no limit).

2.5 Registration by _[date]_ using the registration form.

2.6 Every participating child receives an ice cream voucher.

## 3. The boat

3.1 **Size.** The whole boat, including sails, masts and all parts, must fit on a **DIN A4 footprint (21.0 × 29.7 cm)** and be no higher than **_[42]_ cm**. The boat is checked with the official A4 gauge.

3.2 **Self-built.** The boat must be designed and built by the registered builder(s).
- Not allowed: kits, bought boats, downloaded or copied plans, boats from previous events.
- Allowed: learning building principles from tutorials, books or others; being inspired and then building your own version.
- Adults may help children with safety and with physically very hard steps (e.g. sawing thick wood). The ideas and the design must come from the child.

3.3 **Materials.** Only materials from the green and yellow lists of the [Materials traffic light](../wiki/05-Materials-Traffic-Light.md) are allowed. Yellow materials must be reused and firmly attached. Materials should be donated, collected as litter or found in nature.

3.4 **Forbidden:** Styrofoam/polystyrene, glitter, balloons, batteries, motors, electronics, solvent-based paints or varnish, oil or grease, loose small parts.

3.5 **Propulsion.** The boat may only be moved by the current and the wind.

3.6 **Name.** Each boat has a name. Builders present it at registration.

3.7 **Marking.** Each boat carries its start number, visible and firmly attached (provided at registration).

## 4. Check-in

4.1 All boats pass the **gauge check** (size) and the **material check** at registration.

4.2 Each boat is **weighed, measured and photographed** at check-in. These values count for the prizes.

4.3 A boat that does not pass may be changed at the repair station and checked again before its first heat. It is then weighed and measured again.

4.4 Each builder (or guardian) signs the **builder's pledge**.

## 5. The race

5.1 Boats race in heats of 4–6 boats. The heat plan is posted at the race office.

5.2 **Course.** The course includes chicanes set up by sponsors. Chicanes are part of the course.

5.3 **Start.** Boats are placed upstream of the start gate. The starter opens the gate for all boats at once.

5.4 **Finish.** A boat has finished when its front passes the finish line. The finish judges decide the order.

5.5 **Times.** In round 1, the finish time of every boat is recorded. A boat that has not finished after _[10]_ minutes gets "did not finish" (DNF).

5.6 The first **two** boats of each heat go to the next round. _[Adjust to number of boats.]_ A lucky loser heat may give non-qualified boats a second chance.

5.7 **During the race, builders must not touch their boats**, blow at them or influence them in any way.

5.8 **Stuck boats** may only be freed by official course helpers ("ravens") with a raven stick. A boat that is stuck for more than 2 minutes, or sinks, does not finish this heat.

5.9 **Repairs** between heats are allowed at the repair station. The boat must still meet all rules in section 3.

5.10 If a boat loses parts, the builder helps to recover them after the heat.

## 6. Prizes

6.1 **Fixed prizes:**
- **Fastest Raven:** winner of the final (per age group)
- **Audience Raven:** most talers in the audience vote

6.2 **Sponsor prizes:** Sponsors choose the win condition of their prize (e.g. slowest boat to finish, heaviest boat). All prizes and their win conditions are published by _[date]_.

6.3 Unless stated otherwise, a boat must **finish round 1** to win a prize.

6.4 **Audience vote:** Every participant receives one gold taler, every spectator one silver taler. Both count as one vote. One taler per person. Talers go into the container with the number of the chosen boat. You may not vote for your own boat.

6.5 **Ties** are decided by _[the audience vote / lot]_.

6.6 Every participant receives a certificate.

6.7 The jury consists of _[3]_ people. Its decisions are final.

## 7. Nature and safety

7.1 **Leave no trace.** Nothing stays in the creek. All boats are recovered at the catch net. All chicanes are removed on the same day.

7.2 Only official helpers enter the water.

7.3 Children under 10 are accompanied by an adult near the water.

7.4 Follow the instructions of the organisers. In case of thunderstorm or high water, the race is stopped or postponed.

7.5 Stay on paths and marked banks. Respect plants and animals.

## 8. Fair play

8.1 Be kind. Cheer for all boats, especially those that sink.

8.2 Breaking the rules on purpose can lead to disqualification. The organisers decide.

8.3 Protests go to the race office within 10 minutes after the heat. The jury decides.

## 9. Photos and data

9.1 Every boat is photographed at check-in. Photos and videos are also taken during the event. Details and consent: see the registration form.

9.2 Personal data is only used to organise this event. _[Name of the person responsible, contact.]_
