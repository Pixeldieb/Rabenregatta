# 3. Hull Shapes

The **hull** is the body of the boat, the part that sits in the water. Its shape decides how fast, how stable and how steady your boat is.

## 🤔 Wonder

Why are almost all boats pointed at the front?

## 🧪 Try

You need: two pieces of wood of the same size, a saw or knife (with help), a rain gutter or a long tray of water, a hose or jug.

1. Leave one piece as a rectangle. Shape the other one into a point at the front.
2. Let both float in the same current (rain gutter with running water) or pull them through the water on a string.
3. Which one is faster? Which one goes straighter?

## 💡 Understand

When a boat moves, it has to **push water out of the way**. A flat front pushes a lot of water at once, creating a "wall" of water in front of it. A pointed front **splits** the water gently to the sides. This means less resistance and more speed.

In a creek there's one more thing: **the current**. It drags on everything underwater. A boat with a larger underwater part is carried better by the current, but it also hits stones more often.

## 🛶 Hull types

| Type | Shape | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Raft** | Flat, wide | Very stable, simple, shallow water | Slow, turns around easily |
| **Monohull** | One pointed hull | Fast, goes straight | Can tip over, needs a keel or width |
| **Catamaran** | Two narrow hulls, connected | Very stable, fast | Connection must be strong |
| **Trimaran** | One middle hull, two small side floats | Stable and fast | More parts to build |
| **Round / bowl** | Circular | Turns but doesn't get stuck | Slow, spins |

## 🚤 Use it for your boat

- **Front:** pointed or rounded. It helps the boat slide past stones instead of getting stuck.
- **Back:** can be flat.
- **Bottom:** a smooth bottom glides better. Knots and nails sticking out slow the boat down and catch on things.
- **Going straight:** a long, narrow boat or a small fin at the back (like a feather on an arrow) keeps the boat pointed downstream.
- **Stones and branches:** fewer sharp corners means fewer places to get caught.
