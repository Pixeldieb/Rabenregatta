# After-Event Checklist

## Same day

- [ ] **Catch net removed**. Don't leave it overnight
- [ ] Clean-up walk along the whole course and 50 m below the catch net
- [ ] Recovered items counted: boats started ____ / boats recovered ____
- [ ] Start and finish boards removed, stakes pulled, holes closed
- [ ] Banks checked: anything trampled or damaged?
- [ ] Rubbish sorted and taken away
- [ ] Forgotten boats: reusable parts back into boxes, rest sorted for recycling
- [ ] All boxes counted back in. Missing / broken items noted in the kit logbook

## Within one week

- [ ] Wood and boxes dried and stored
- [ ] Consumables list for next time
- [ ] Press report with photos sent (only photos with consent) → [press release template](../marketing/press-release.md)
- [ ] Results and thanks posted online and in the village newsletter
- [ ] **Thank-you notes** to landowner, municipality, fire brigade, supporters, helpers
- [ ] Borrowed items returned
- [ ] Surveys evaluated (see [Evaluation](../wiki/12-Evaluation.md))

## Within four weeks

- [ ] Team review meeting: what worked, what to change?
- [ ] Personal data deleted as promised on the registration form
- [ ] Final budget
- [ ] Date for next year pencilled in
- [ ] **Event report shared** in `wiki/reports/`. See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
