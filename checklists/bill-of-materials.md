# Bill of Materials (BOM)

Everything you need for a Rabenregatta, in one list. Use it to plan, borrow, buy and ask sponsors.

**Sized for:** about **20 builders at the same time** at the building stations, **30–60 boats** and **100–300 spectators**. Scale up or down from there.

**Columns:**

- **Type:** **R** = reusable (buy or build once, use for years), **C** = consumable (refill every event)
- **Source:** where it usually comes from. **Borrow** = club, fire brigade, school, municipality · **Donate** = collected or given · **Build** = make it yourselves · **Buy**
- Box numbers refer to the [Eurobox system](../wiki/06-Eurobox-System.md). The detailed box contents with count columns are in the [packing lists](eurobox-packing-lists.md).

**Golden rule:** Borrow before you buy. Many villages already own tents, benches and tables. A complete kit can travel from village to village.

## What to buy first

With a small budget, prioritise like this:

1. **Safety:** first aid kit, safety glasses, gloves
2. **Core tools:** coping saws, hand drills, awls, clamps, scissors
3. **Race course:** planks, stakes, cord (catch net borrowed)
4. **Race office:** cloakroom tickets, kitchen scale, stopwatches (phones work too)
5. **Euroboxes:** start with what you have; buy boxes over time
6. **Banner:** once there's a sponsor for it

Everything else: borrow, build or ask a sponsor. See the [budget template](../templates/budget.md).
