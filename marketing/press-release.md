# Press Releases

Two templates: an announcement before the event and a report afterwards. Local papers like short, concrete texts with a good photo and a quote.

---

## 1. Announcement (send 4–6 weeks before)

**Headline:** Boats from corks and cartons: Rabenregatta on the _[creek name]_

**Subheading:** _[Village]_ invites everyone to build their own boat and race it on _[date]_

_[Village], [date]._ – Young and old are invited to build a boat that fits on a sheet of A4 paper. There are three conditions: build it yourself, use reused or natural materials, and race it on the _[creek name]_ on _[date]_. The Rabenregatta starts at _[time]_ at _[place]_. Participation is free for all children and teenagers, and every child gets an ice cream.

"Everyone can build a boat. And when it floats, you know: I can do it," says _[name, role]_ of the organising team. "That's what it's about: the experience of creating something yourself."

Beforehand, there are free build workshops with tools and materials on _[dates]_ at _[place]_. Styrofoam, glitter and batteries are not allowed. Every boat is fished out of the creek after the race. Until _[date]_, the organisers are collecting corks, drink cartons and wood offcuts at _[collection points]_.

Local businesses are part of the race: each sponsor sets up a themed obstacle on the course and decides what their prize is for. The dog school, for example, hangs dog leashes into the water and awards a prize for the slowest boat to reach the finish. There are also prizes for the fastest, the heaviest and the tallest boat, an audience prize, and a certificate for every participant. The event is organised by _[organisers]_ with the support of _[supporters]_.

**Registration and information:** _[contact, website]_

**Press contact:** _[name, phone, e-mail]_

## 2. Report (send within 2 days after)

**Headline:** _[Number]_ home-made boats race down the _[creek name]_

**Subheading:** Rabenregatta in _[village]_: _[winner's boat name]_ wins, but the creek stays clean

_[Village], [date]._ – _[Number]_ boats made of corks, drink cartons, wood offcuts and old T-shirts raced down the _[creek name]_ on Saturday. _[Number]_ builders aged _[youngest]_ to _[oldest]_ had designed and built them themselves. About _[number]_ spectators cheered along the banks.

The fastest boat was _[boat name]_ by _[first name, age]_. _[One sentence on what was special about it.]_ The Underdog Prize of the dog school for the slowest boat to finish went to _[boat name]_, which needed _[time]_ and made it through the leash curtain. The audience chose _[boat name]_ as their favourite with _[number]_ talers.

"_[Quote from a child builder, e.g. about what went wrong and how they fixed it]_," said _[first name, age]_.

Before the race, _[number]_ children and adults had built their boats in _[number]_ workshops. In the weeks before, _[village]_ residents collected _[kg]_ of material. After the race, all _[number]_ boats were recovered from the creek. Not a single piece was left behind.

_[Name]_ of the organising team: "_[Quote about the community / next year]_."

**Photo caption:** _[Description, photographer]_ (only use photos with consent)

---

**Tip:** Mention **numbers** (boats, builders, kg collected, 100% recovered). Editors love them.
