Dear _[name]_,

on _[date]_, the Rabenregatta takes place on the _[creek name]_ in _[village]_. We warmly invite your pupils and their families to take part.

**The idea:** Every participant builds their own boat that fits on an A4 sheet, using reused and natural materials. Then all boats race down the creek.

**Why it fits into school:**

- **Science:** buoyancy, density, stability, wind (Archimedes' principle in practice)
- **Craft and technology:** planning, sawing, drilling, lashing, testing, improving
- **Sustainability:** reuse, the waste hierarchy, protecting water bodies
- **Personal development:** children experience that their own ideas and effort work. Educational psychologists call this self-efficacy ([Bandura, 1997](../wiki/Sources.md#bandura-1997)).

**What we offer:**

- A free build workshop at your school, with tools and materials (Eurobox kit) and helpers. Duration: about 2.5 hours
- Free tutorials and worksheets on the physics of floating
- Participation is free for all pupils, and every child gets an ice cream voucher

- Put up a material collection box at school
- If possible: a workshop date between _[date]_ and _[date]_
