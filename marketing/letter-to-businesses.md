**Rabenregatta _[year]_: Would you like to build a chicane with us?**

Dear _[name]_,

on _[date]_, children, families and neighbours will race home-made boats down the _[creek name]_. Each boat fits on a sheet of A4 paper and is made from donated materials, collected litter or things from nature. Taking part is **free for all children and teenagers**, and every child gets an ice cream.

We are not looking for a logo on a banner. We are looking for **businesses from our village that want to be part of the race**. Here's how:

**1. You give a prize.** For example vouchers or goods for the prize bags.

**2. You decide what it's for.** The fastest boat? The slowest? The heaviest, the tallest, the most colourful? You choose the win condition, and your prize carries your name.

**3. You build a chicane.** An obstacle on the course that fits your business. The local dog school, for example, hangs dog leashes into the water, and the boats have to find their way through the "leash curtain".

**4. Optional: a workshop with your team.** For example, cast a symbol of your business in concrete and paint it together. It becomes your chicane, and afterwards it can go into your shop window.

**What you get:** a sign at your chicane, your name on the prize, a moment on stage at the award ceremony, photos of the boats passing your chicane, and above all a great day with the village.

We'd love to visit you and develop your idea together. I'll call you in the next few days, or just get in touch.

Kind regards,
