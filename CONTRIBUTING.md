# Contributing

Thank you for helping! Rabenregatta gets better every time a community runs it and shares what they learned.

## What helps most

- **Event reports** – What worked in your village? What did not? Add a short report to `wiki/reports/` (e.g. `2027-ourvillage.md`).
- **Better templates** – clearer rules, better forms, new checklists.
- **Translations** – copy a file, add a language suffix (e.g. `official-rules.de.md`), translate it.
- **Equipment ideas** – a better start board, a better catch net, nice bunting. Photos and drawings are welcome.
- **Tutorials** – explain a building principle. Please explain *principles*, not step-by-step plans for a whole boat. Participants must design their own.
- **Sources** – found a good study? Add it to [wiki/Sources.md](wiki/Sources.md).

## How to contribute

**Without Git:** Open an [issue](../../issues) and describe your idea, or attach your file. We will add it.

**With Git:**

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a branch: `git checkout -b my-improvement`.
3. Make your change in Markdown.
4. Open a pull request and describe what you changed and why.

## Writing style

- **Easy language.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Explain technical words.
- **Friendly and direct.** Speak to the reader as "you".
- **Back up claims.** If you say something is proven, link the source.
- **Metric units.** Centimetres, grams, litres.
- **No personal data** of participants in reports or photos without consent.

## Photos

Only upload photos where everyone shown agreed to publication. For children, you need consent from their guardians. When in doubt, use photos of boats, not faces.
