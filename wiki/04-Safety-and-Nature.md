# Safety and Nature

Rabenregatta is a family event near water, with tools. A little planning keeps everyone safe and the creek healthy.

## People

### Water

- **Children under 10 are always accompanied** by an adult near the water.
- Mark a **spectator zone**. Keep people away from steep or slippery banks.
- **Only designated helpers** (the "ravens") go into the creek. They wear rubber boots or waders and a high-visibility vest.
- Keep a **throw line** or rescue pole at the start and at the finish.
- Check the water level on the morning of the event. **If the water is too high or too fast, the race is postponed.** Set this rule in advance.

### Tools

- Give a short **tool briefing** at every workshop: how to hold a saw, where your other hand goes, gloves and safety glasses when drilling or sawing.
- Children only use tools that fit their age, under supervision.
- **No hot glue guns, no power tools** for children. Adults may use them at a separate, supervised station.
- Knives only with a blunt tip, and only after a briefing.

### Health

- **First aid kit** at the workshop and at the creek (in Germany, for example, to DIN 13157). One helper with first aid training at each station.
- Know the **emergency number** and the exact address and access point for the ambulance.
- Water, shade and sunscreen in summer. Ask about allergies (wasps, food).
- Check for **ticks** after the event and tell parents to do the same.
- **Hygiene:** wash hands after contact with creek water, especially before eating. Put up a hand-washing station.

### Weather

- Thunderstorm = **immediate stop**. Everyone leaves the creek.
- Decide on the rain plan 48 hours before. Tell everyone how you will announce it (website, WhatsApp group, radio).

## Nature

The creek is not just our race track. It is home to fish, insects, amphibians, birds and plants.

### Rules for the creek

1. **Leave no trace.** Every boat, every piece of string, every cork comes out. The catch net is mandatory.
2. **No problematic materials** in the water. See [Materials traffic light](05-Materials-Traffic-Light.md).
3. **Don't block the creek.** Boards lie across the creek, not in it. Water and fish must be able to pass.
4. **Protect the banks.** Use fixed access points. Don't trample plants.
5. **Check the season.** Avoid breeding and spawning times at sensitive spots. Ask your nature conservation authority or local nature group.
6. **Small groups in the water.** Only helpers step in, and only where necessary.

### Why no microplastics?

Plastic breaks down into tiny pieces called microplastics. Animals swallow them, and they stay in the environment for a very long time ([GESAMP, 2015](Sources.md#gesamp-2015)). Styrofoam and glitter are especially bad, because they break into tiny pieces right away and are almost impossible to collect.

### After the race

- A **clean-up walk** along the whole course and 50 m below the catch net.
- Count what was fished out. It makes a great story for the press: *"42 boats started, 42 boats recovered, 0 pieces left behind."*

## Safety concept on one page

Before the event, write a one-page safety concept:

- [ ] Map with danger spots, first aid, emergency access
- [ ] Emergency number, address, name of the person in charge
- [ ] Maximum water level for racing (e.g. a mark on a stake)
- [ ] Who is responsible for which zone
- [ ] Thunderstorm and rain plan

> **Note:** Duty of supervision, liability and insurance depend on local law. This page is not legal advice. Please clarify these points with your municipality, club insurer or a qualified advisor.
