# Materials Traffic Light

What may go into a Rabenregatta boat? We use a simple traffic light.

**Basic idea:** First use what already exists (reuse). Choose natural materials. Make sure nothing can break off into the creek.

## 🟢 Green: go ahead

Natural or harmless materials. Best choice.

| Material | Tips |
|---|---|
| Untreated wood, offcuts, bark, driftwood | Light woods (spruce, pine, poplar, balsa offcuts) float best |
| Wine corks (natural cork) | Excellent floats. Collect them in restaurants |
| Bamboo, reed, dry sticks | Hollow reed floats well. Only collect where allowed |
| Paper, cardboard | Only as sails or decoration. Soaks through quickly in water |
| Natural string (cotton, jute, hemp, sisal) | For lashing parts together |
| Cotton or linen fabric scraps | Sails |
| Beeswax, natural resin | Makes wood and paper water-repellent |
| Wooden dowels, toothpicks, skewers, clothes pegs | Joints and masts |
| Starch paste, casein glue | Home-made, harmless glue |

## 🟡 Yellow: allowed if reused and firmly attached

Not ideal for nature, but fine if they are **reused** (not bought new) and **cannot break off**.

| Material | Condition |
|---|---|
| Plastic bottles, caps, containers | Reused, intact, firmly attached. No cut-up small pieces |
| Drink cartons (Tetra Pak etc.) | Reused, rinsed, cut edges sealed or folded |
| Rubber bands | Firmly attached, count them out and back in |
| Screws, nails, wire | Not sharp-ended on the outside |
| Wood glue (PVA, water-resistant D3) | Small amounts, fully dry before the race |
| Water-based paints | Fully dry. No flaking |
| Old fabric with synthetic fibres | Only as a sail, well hemmed |
| Aluminium foil, tins | Folded edges, no sharp parts |

## 🔴 Red: not allowed

These break into tiny pieces, are toxic or are impossible to collect.

| Material | Why |
|---|---|
| **Styrofoam / polystyrene (EPS, XPS)** | Crumbles into microplastic immediately |
| **Glitter, sequins, confetti** | Microplastic by design |
| **Balloons** | Burst into pieces, deadly for animals |
| **Batteries, motors, electronics** | Toxic materials. Also: boats are powered by nature only |
| **Solvent-based paints, varnish, spray paint** | Toxic for water life |
| **Oil, grease, petroleum jelly** | Forms films on water |
| **Hot glue in large amounts, silicone** | Plastic that peels off |
| **Loose small parts** (beads, small plastic toys) | Get lost in the creek |
| **Anything new bought only for the boat that is not green** | Against the spirit of the event |

## When in doubt

Ask at the **material check** (at the workshop or at registration). The jury decides. Tip: if you are not sure, choose something from the green list.

## Where to find materials

- **Home:** corks, cartons, bottles, old T-shirts, wooden spoons, clothes pegs
- **Carpenter, joiner, sawmill:** wood offcuts (often free and gladly given)
- **Restaurants, wine bars:** corks
- **Recycling centre, repair café:** ask what they have
- **Nature:** fallen sticks, reed, bark. Only collect what is lying on the ground and where it is allowed

Set up a **material collection** before the event. Clean, sort and store the materials in the [Euroboxes](06-Eurobox-System.md).
