# Documenting the Boats

**Take pictures of every boat!** A boat lasts one summer. A photo lasts forever.

## Why it matters

- **Pride:** A photo of your own boat is proof: *"I built that."* It keeps the self-efficacy experience alive long after race day ([Bandura, 1997](Sources.md#bandura-1997)).
- **Inspiration:** Next year's builders see what is possible. This is what Bandura calls *vicarious experience*: "Someone like me did that." Photos inspire; they are not blueprints. Last year's boats can't race again.
- **Prizes:** Weight, height and materials are recorded at check-in. The photo shows the boat as it started.
- **Sponsors:** They love photos of boats passing *their* chicane.
- **The press:** A good boat photo is often the best picture for the local paper.
- **This kit:** Photos help other villages. See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## The photo station

Set up a small **photo station** right next to the check-in. Every boat stops there before its first heat.

**You need:**

- A table with a **neutral background**: a light sheet of fabric or a large board
- An **A4 grid mat**: an A4 frame drawn on the table cover, with a 1 cm grid. It shows the size at a glance
- The **start number card** next to the boat in each photo
- A camera or phone, ideally on a small tripod, always at the same distance
- Good light: outdoors in the shade, or a white umbrella against direct sun
- One **photographer** responsible for the station

**Take three photos per boat:**

1. **Side view** at water level (table level)
2. **Top view** on the A4 grid mat
3. **Detail**: the cleverest part, chosen by the builder (*"What should we photograph?"* is a great question!)

Optional: a fourth photo of **builder and boat together**, only with photo consent.

## Record the boat data

At the same time, the check-in team fills in the [boat register](../templates/boat-register.md):

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Start number, boat name, builders, age group | Basic data |
| **Weight** (kitchen scale, grams) | For the "heaviest" or "lightest" prize |
| **Height** (A4 gauge / ruler, cm) | For the "tallest" prize and the rule check |
| Main materials | For reuse prizes and statistics |
| Photo numbers | To match photos and boats |

## During the race

- One person photographs **each chicane** as the boats pass. These are the most exciting pictures.
- Short slow-motion video at the **finish line**. It also helps judges with close finishes.
- The **award ceremony**: winners with their boats (with consent).

## File names and archive

Name the files the same way every time:

```text
YEAR-village-boat-NUMBER-view.jpg
2027-ourvillage-boat-017-side.jpg
2027-ourvillage-boat-017-top.jpg
2027-ourvillage-chicane-dogschool-01.jpg
```

Store them in a shared folder straight after the event, together with the boat register. For this repository, choose the best ones (boats only), shrink them and add them to `assets/images/events/YEAR-village/`.

## Privacy

- **Boats, hands and chicanes** can almost always be photographed without problems.
- **Faces** only with consent. See [photo consent](../templates/photo-consent.md).
- People who don't want to be photographed get a coloured sticker or wristband. The photographer knows the colour.
- No full names on published photos, only first names or boat names.

> **Note:** Photo and privacy rules differ by country (e.g. GDPR in the EU). This page is not legal advice.

## Give something back

- Send each family the photos of their boat (e.g. by e-mail link, only their own boat).
- Print a photo on the **certificate**, if you can print on site or send the certificates later.
- Make a small **boat gallery** for the village: in the town hall, the school or online.
