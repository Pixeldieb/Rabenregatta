# Race Format

This page explains how the race works: age groups, heats, the final and the prizes. The binding version is in the [official rules](../rules/official-rules.md).

## Age groups

| Group | Age | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 🐣 **Raven chicks** | 6–9 | An adult may help with safety and tools, never with ideas or building |
| 🐦 **Young ravens** | 10–13 | Build alone or in teams of up to 3 |
| 🐦‍⬛ **Ravens** | 14 and over | Teens and adults |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Raven families** | Mixed | Families or mixed teams; the youngest member has the final say on the design |

Adapt the groups to the number of participants. With fewer than 20 boats, one group plus a family class is often enough.

## Heats and final

Boats race in **heats** of 4–6 boats. The first two in each heat go through to the next round. The best boats race in the **final**.

**Example with 24 boats in one age group:**

1. **Round 1:** 4 heats of 6 boats → the first 2 go through (8 boats)
2. **Semi-finals:** 2 heats of 4 boats → the first 2 go through (4 boats)
3. **Final:** 4 boats → places 1–3

Use the [heat plan template](../templates/heat-plan.md).

### Second chance

Boats that did not qualify may race in a **"lucky loser" heat**. Its winner gets the last place in the next round. Everyone likes a second chance.

### If a boat gets stuck

- Only the **ravens** (helpers with raven sticks) may free a stuck boat. They push it gently back into the current.
- Builders **do not** touch their boats during the race.
- A boat that is still stuck after 2 minutes, or that sinks, is out of this heat. Cheering is allowed!

### If a boat breaks

Builders may repair their boat between heats at the **repair station**, with the materials in the boxes. The boat must still pass the A4 gauge.

## Prizes

Speed is only one way to win. Every participant should have a real chance at something.

| Prize | Criterion | Decided by |
|---|---|---|
| 🏁 **Fastest Raven** | Winner of the final (per age group) | The race |
| 💡 **Clever Raven** | Most creative idea or technique | Jury |
| ♻️ **Treasure Raven** | Best reuse of materials | Jury |
| ⚓ **Iron Raven** | Most seaworthy: survives all heats without repairs | Race office |
| ❤️ **Audience Raven** | Favourite boat of the spectators | Audience vote (e.g. corks in jars) |

Every participant gets a **certificate** with their boat's name. See the jury's [score sheet](../templates/score-sheet.md).

**Tip:** Prizes don't need to cost much. A wooden medal from the carpenter, a raven stamp, a small plant or a voucher from the village shop are great. Avoid plastic trophies.

## Commentary

A good commentator with a megaphone turns a creek into a stadium. Introduce each boat by name and builder before the start. "And here comes *Cork Express* by Mia, 8 years old, built from 23 wine corks!"
