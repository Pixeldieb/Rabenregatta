# Eurobox System

All tools and materials travel in **Euroboxes**: stackable, standard-sized plastic containers (also called Euro containers or EURO boxes). They are sturdy, last for decades, stack neatly and fit on a Euro pallet.

**Colour coding** means anyone can find anything, even new helpers and children who cannot read yet.

## Colour code

| Colour | Contents | Symbol idea |
|---|---|---|
| 🔵 **Blue** | **Tools**: Blue 1 cutting, Blue 2 assembling, Blue 3 power tool station | Hammer |
| 🟢 **Green** | **Natural materials**: wood, cork, bamboo, natural string | Leaf |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | **Reused materials**: bottles, cartons, fabric scraps | Recycling arrows |
| ⚪ **Grey** | **Connectors and consumables**: rubber bands, dowels, nails, glue, sandpaper | Screw |
| 🔴 **Red** | **Safety**: first aid, safety glasses, gloves, sunscreen | Cross |
| ⚫ **Black** | **Race office** (Black 1: tickets, forms, scale, stopwatches) and **gallery** (Black 2: talers, voting containers, photo station) | Flag |

Euroboxes come in these colours from most suppliers. If you only have grey boxes, use coloured tape or labels on all four sides.

## Sizes

The base sizes are standardised. The most useful for Rabenregatta:

| Base (L × W) | Height | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 600 × 400 mm | 220 mm | Tools, larger materials |
| 400 × 300 mm | 120 mm | Small parts, connectors, first aid |
| 600 × 400 mm | 320 mm | Wood offcuts, bottles |

Tip: Lids protect against rain and dust. Hinged lids (on "Euro stacking containers with lid") cannot get lost.

## Labelling

Every box gets:

1. A **number** (e.g. "Blue 1", "Blue 2")
2. A **content list** in a sleeve on the front (see [packing lists](../checklists/eurobox-packing-lists.md))
3. A **"home" line**: who owns it, where it is stored

For tool boxes, a **shadow board** in the box is very helpful. A foam insert or drawn outline shows where each tool belongs. Missing tools are visible at a glance.

| Box | Quantity |
|---|---|
| 🔵 Blue 1 + 2: hand tools | 2 × 600 × 400 × 220 |
| 🔵 Blue 3: power tool station | 1 × 600 × 400 × 320 |
| 🟢 Green: natural materials | 2 × 600 × 400 × 320 |
| 🟡 Yellow: reused materials | 2 × 600 × 400 × 320 |
| ⚪ Grey: connectors | 2 × 400 × 300 × 120 |
| 🔴 Red: safety | 1 × 400 × 300 × 120 |
| ⚫ Black 1: race office | 1 × 600 × 400 × 220 |
| ⚫ Black 2: gallery, vote, photo station | 1 × 600 × 400 × 320 |

**Total: 12 boxes.** They fit in a car boot or on a hand truck. Everything else (tents, tables, race course, banner) is in the [bill of materials](../checklists/bill-of-materials.md).

## Share the kit

A full kit is a great thing to **lend between villages**. One kit can travel through a whole region. Keep a logbook in the black box: date, village, what was used or broken.

## Rhythm: out, use, back

- **Before:** check against the content list. Refill consumables.
- **During:** each box stays at its colour-coded station.
- **After:** count tools back in. Note missing or broken items. Dry everything before closing the lids.
