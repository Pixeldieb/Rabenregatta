# Evaluation

Did it work? A simple evaluation helps you improve your next event. It also helps other villages decide whether to try it, and it gives funders facts.

## What to count (easy)

| Figure | How |
|---|---|
| Number of boats and builders | Registration list |
| Age distribution | Registration (age group only) |
| Workshop participants | Attendance list |
| Spectators (estimate) | Count at a peak moment, multiply by the length of the course |
| Materials collected (kg) | Weigh the collection boxes |
| Boats recovered / boats started | Catch net team. Goal: 100% |
| Helpers and supporting organisations | Team list |

## What to ask (medium)

A short, **anonymous** survey after the race. See [feedback survey template](../templates/feedback-survey.md).

- For **children**: 4–5 questions with smiley faces
- For **teens and adults**: short questions with a 1–4 scale plus two open questions

## Measuring self-efficacy (advanced)

If you want to measure a change in self-efficacy, ask the same questions **before** (at registration or the first workshop) and **after** the event.

A well-known, validated tool is the **General Self-Efficacy Scale** by Schwarzer and Jerusalem ([1995](Sources.md#schwarzer-1995)). It has 10 statements, such as *"I can always manage to solve difficult problems if I try hard enough"*, each answered on a 4-point scale. It is available in many languages from the authors' website and is suitable for teenagers and adults.

**Be careful with conclusions:**

- A one-day event will probably cause only small changes on a general scale.
- Without a comparison group, you cannot say for sure that the event caused the change.
- The questions in our children's survey are simple and **not validated**. They show a tendency, nothing more.

For a solid study, partner with a university or teacher training college. Student theses are a great option.

## Data protection

- Collect surveys **anonymously**. Do not ask for names.
- To match "before" and "after" answers, use a self-made code (e.g. first letter of mother's name + day of birth).
- Only collect what you need. Delete raw data after evaluation.
- For children, get guardians' consent for surveys.

> **Note:** Data protection rules (e.g. GDPR in the EU) apply. This page is not legal advice.
