# FAQ

### What does "Rabenregatta" mean?

*Rabe* is German for raven, and a *regatta* is a boat race. Ravens are famous problem solvers and tool users, just like our builders.

### Does it cost anything to take part?

We recommend keeping it **free**. Materials are collected or donated. If you need money, ask sponsors rather than participants.

### We don't have a creek. What now?

Use a ditch, a small river with a calm side arm, a mill race or an open channel. If there's no flowing water, use a long gutter or rain channel on a slight slope with a garden hose, or hold a **wind race** on a pond or a paddling pool, with sails only.

### What is the youngest age?

Around 6 years for building with tools under supervision. Younger children can join a family team.

### May parents help?

With **safety** and with **physically hard steps** (like sawing thick wood), yes. With **ideas and design**, no. See [Build workshops](10-Build-Workshops.md#the-helpers-role-ask-dont-do).

### What counts as a "previous design"?

Kits, downloaded plans, a copy of someone else's boat, and last year's boat. Learning principles from tutorials and being inspired is fine. When in doubt, the builder should be able to explain **why** the boat is built the way it is.

### How do you check whether a boat is self-built?

We trust people, and we ask. The jury talks to builders: "How did you get this idea? What didn't work?" Builders who made their boat can answer easily. The **builder's pledge** ([template](../templates/builder-pledge.md)) makes the expectation clear.

### Is a boat with sails allowed?

Yes. Wind and current are allowed. Motors, batteries and pushing are not.

### Does the boat have to carry anything?

Not in the basic rules. As an option, you can add a **cargo challenge**: every boat carries a standard object (e.g. an egg-sized stone or a small wooden raven) to the finish.

### How many people do we need?

A team of 3–5 for planning and 10–15 helpers on race day for 30–50 boats.

### What does the equipment cost?

Most of it can be borrowed or donated. If you buy everything new, expect a few hundred euros, mostly for the Euroboxes and tools, which last many years. See the [budget template](../templates/budget.md).

### Can we run it as a school project?

Absolutely. Rabenregatta covers physics (buoyancy, stability, wind), craft and technology, art and sustainability. Build during lessons, race at the school festival.

### What happens to the boats afterwards?

The builders take them home. They are proud of them! Boats that are left behind: reusable parts go back into the material boxes, the rest is sorted for recycling.
