# Venue and Creek

The creek is your race track. A good spot makes everything easier.

## The ideal creek

| Feature | Ideal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Width** | 1–4 m | Boards can span it; boats stay close together |
| **Depth** | Up to about 30 cm on the course | Safer for children; helpers can step in with boots |
| **Current** | Gentle and steady, walking pace or slower | Boats move, but helpers can keep up |
| **Course length** | 20–100 m | Long enough for excitement, short enough to watch |
| **Obstacles** | A few stones and bends | Makes it interesting. Too many and every boat gets stuck |
| **Banks** | Flat, firm, accessible | Spectators can walk along; helpers can reach the water |
| **Downstream** | A calm spot for the catch net | Nothing escapes |

## The surroundings

- [ ] Space for **building stations** (tent or tables), the race office and the **gallery**, near the start
- [ ] Space for spectators along the course, away from steep banks
- [ ] Shade, seating, a meeting point
- [ ] Toilets (nearby building or rented)
- [ ] Parking and access for emergency vehicles
- [ ] Power outlet (optional, for PA system)
- [ ] A **rain alternative** nearby (barn, hall, fire station)

## Walk the course before you decide

Go there **twice**: once in normal weather and once after rain. Water levels can change a lot.

On your walk, check:

1. Where can the **start board** lie securely?
2. Where is the **finish line**? Can spectators see it?
3. Where does the **catch net** go? Is the water calm there?
4. Where are the **danger spots**: steep banks, deep pools, drains, weirs, broken glass?
5. Are there **sensitive spots**: nesting birds, spawning areas, rare plants?

Draw a simple map. Mark start, finish, catch net, spectator zone, first aid, workshop tent and toilets. Put it in `assets/` and on the helper briefing sheet.

## Permissions

Who owns the land and the water, and who needs to agree, is different in every country and region. Typical contacts are:

- the **landowner** of the banks
- the **municipality** (for public land and events)
- the **water authority** (in Germany: *Untere Wasserbehörde*)
- the **nature conservation authority** (in Germany: *Untere Naturschutzbehörde*), especially if the area is protected
- the local **fishing club** or fishing rights holder

Contact them early and explain the event. Usually people like the idea. Bring a short description and the map.

> **Note:** This page is not legal advice. Please ask your municipality which permissions apply to you.
